A permanent opening is a gap in the wall with no door — marked by two pale jamb ticks and a break in the seam. None can ever be closed:
So the Observation Deck has no door of its own: sealing it means closing the Living Quarters door, which seals both together. Storage is the mirror case — sealing it means sealing the whole service corridor.
Doors inside a sealed volume stay open. Sealing a place means closing its boundary, not everything within it. That is why sealing the service sector leaves the Engineering, Hangar Bay 4 and Hangar Bay 5 doors open — those bays are inside the sealed volume. Pick a target below and the caption lists exactly which doors fall into which category.
