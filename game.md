## Environment

- A space station
- 5 Hangar doors leading to the outside of the station
- Hallways network leading to rooms and hangar doors with 10 labeled hallway doors

## Player actions

Very simple and limited actions that the player can perform in the game.

- Read / hear messages from other actors
- Open or close hangar doors
- Open or close hallway doors

## Actors

- **The security operators**: need to enter and exit the hangar to perform their duties at frequent intervals.
- **TBD**: ...

## Everyday events

Those events are not linked to a special event, they are just part of the normal operation of the station and are frequent.

- A citizen needs to have a door open to enter or exit the hangar.
- ...

## Special events

Linked events that will occur in the game and have multiple actors involved.
Multiple events with progress simultaneously.

### The construction of an extension

A construction team is working on an extension of the station.

- Hangar doors need to be opened and closed at certain times to allow the construction team to enter and exit the hangar.
- An hallway door needs to be closed to allow to avoid loss of pressure in the station while the construction team is working on the extension.
- Some info about the construction is sent periodically
- A timer to reopen the hallway door is given, but is delayed multiple times

### Invaders attack the station

An event that will occur towards the end of the game involving all actors.

- A squad of security operators goes out to explore outside the station
- They Report for a ship found empty of crew and damaged, an accident?
- They have detected enemies and ask all dors to be shut down after certain time, to leave time for people to get back inside
- A hangar doors are forced open, they need to be closed again quickly
- Some hallway doors need to be closed, other doors need to be opened
- The event ends after the invaders are defeated and the hangar doors are closed again

## Keeper challenge

At the end of the game and at regular intervals, the player will be asked to answer a question about why a door was opened or closed, or if a door can be opened or closed. The player will have to answer correctly to continue the game. They need to keep track of the events and the actions of the actors to answer correctly.
